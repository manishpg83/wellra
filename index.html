FAQ

What types of fruits can I juice with the Wellra PureSqueeze?
The PureSqueeze is built for citrus — oranges, lemons, limes, and grapefruits of nearly any size, with no adapters needed.
Do I need to peel the fruit before juicing?
No peeling needed — simply cut the fruit in half and place it in the chute. The rind stays out of your glass.
Is it easy to clean?
Yes. Four removable parts are all dishwasher safe, so clean-up takes about a minute.
What is the motor power?
PureSqueeze uses a professional-grade induction motor for consistent, powerful squeezing.
Is the juicer made with safe materials?
All food-contact components are made from 100% BPA-free, food-safe materials.
Can Wellra juice apples or carrots?
PureSqueeze is optimized specifically for citrus fruits and is not designed for hard produce like apples or carrots.
